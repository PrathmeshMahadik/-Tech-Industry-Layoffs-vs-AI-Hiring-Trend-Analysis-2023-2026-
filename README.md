
# **Tech Industry Layoffs vs AI Hiring Trend Analysis (2023–2026)**

## **Technology Industry Layoffs & AI Hiring Insights**

## **Project Objective**

The objective of this project is to analyze **technology industry layoffs, AI hiring, overall technology hiring, AI job growth, layoff rates, and AI-related layoffs** during the period **2023–2026**.

The project focuses on comparing layoffs and AI hiring across different companies, industries, locations, years, and quarters to identify workforce trends and changing hiring patterns in the technology industry.

The project uses **Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, MySQL, SQL, MySQL Connector/Python, Jupyter Notebook, and Power BI** to perform data cleaning, analysis, visualization, database operations, and dashboard development.
